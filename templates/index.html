How The System Works

A transparent, closed-loop workflow ensuring surplus food is inspected, collected, and delivered without unnecessary delays.

1
Donor Posts Surplus

Donors post edible surplus meals specifying quantity, preparation time, best-before date, and pickup location.

2
Verification & Match

Admins and verified NGOs review availability against expiry guidelines and approve legitimate requests.

3
Volunteer Pickup

An active volunteer accepts the pickup task, collects the food in hygienic containers, and marks status.

4
NGO Distribution

NGO receives the batch, reconciles quantity, and distributes hot meals directly to registered beneficiaries.

Urgent Mission

Why Surplus Food Redistribution Matters

Millions of tons of wholesome, safe, and nutritious food end up in landfills each year while millions of vulnerable families experience hunger. Food waste in landfills produces massive methane emissions that accelerate climate change.

Environmental Preservation

Reduces greenhouse gases generated by decomposing organic waste in municipal landfills.

Direct Social Relief

Channels fresh meals to orphanages, elderly homes, day shelters, and disaster relief zones.

Traceable Accountability

Provides verified donation receipts, distribution history, and complete audit tracking.
