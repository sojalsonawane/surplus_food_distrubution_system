Food Safety & Hygiene Guidelines

Protecting vulnerable health through rigorous data validation and handling standards.

Mandatory Food Safety Warning

“Only safe and edible surplus food should be donated. Do not donate spoiled, contaminated or expired food.”

System Responsibility Disclaimer

Please Note: The Surplus Food Distribution System does not physically inspect, lab-test, or chemically guarantee food safety. The system performs strict software-level data validation (validating preparation times, expiry timestamps, storage units, contact veracity, and volunteer pickup logs) and facilitates coordination between verified parties. Donors and recipient NGOs remain responsible for adhering to local food safety standards and visual inspection prior to consumption.

Acceptable Food Categories

  • Fresh Cooked Meals: Freshly prepared vegetarian or non-vegetarian dishes cooked in hygienic commercial or domestic kitchens within safe consumption windows (typically consumed within 4 to 6 hours if unrefrigerated).
  • Raw Uncooked Grains & Pulses: Sealed rice, lentils, wheat flour, spices, and grains in intact original packaging.
  • Bakery Goods: Unexpired bread, buns, and dry bakery items without perishable creams past safe room temperature limits.
  • Packaged Non-Perishables: Sealed canned goods, tetra-pack milk, biscuits, and dry food rations well ahead of their official expiry dates.
  • Fresh Fruits & Vegetables: Clean, unbruised, and unspoiled produce.

Strictly Prohibited Items

  • Plate Leftovers: Half-eaten or partially consumed food left on banquet plates or dining tables.
  • Spoiled or Sour Smelling Food: Cooked food left at room temperature for extended durations displaying sour aroma, discoloration, or sliminess.
  • Expired Packaged Goods: Any item past its official printed "Use By" or "Best Before" date.
  • Damaged or Swollen Cans: Bulging, rusted, or leaking canned food products indicating microbial fermentation.
  • Alcoholic Beverages or Tobacco: Not permitted under any circumstances.

Automated Data Validation Checks

Every surplus food listing submitted through our portal is subject to programmatic checks before reaching volunteers:

Chronological Validation
Preparation date/time must be strictly earlier than best-before date/time.
Non-Expired Check
Best-before date/time must be in the future at the moment of submission.
Serving & Quantity Bounds
Quantity and estimated servings count must be positive numbers (> 0).
Location Completeness
Donor pickup address, city, area, and valid 6-digit PIN code are required.
Donor Phone Verification
10-digit Indian mobile number format strictly enforced.
Safe Packaging
Instructions must specify safe, food-grade transport packaging.
