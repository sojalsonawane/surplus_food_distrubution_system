How It Works

The complete, step-by-step lifecycle of food donation and distribution.

End-to-End Distribution Workflow

Our platform enforces a strict finite state machine to guarantee that food is systematically tracked, preserved, and distributed without safety compromises.

1
Step 1: Donation Creation
Donor Posts Surplus Food

A registered individual, hotel, or caterer posts details including food category, quantity, number of estimated servings, preparation date/time, and mandatory best-before date/time.

Initial Status: Pending
2
Step 2: Verification & Approval
Admin / NGO Verification

The system checks that the food has not expired, the pickup address is valid, and the donor contact number is verified. The listing is approved and made visible to verified NGOs.

Status: Approved
3
Step 3: Volunteer Assignment
Volunteer Assigned for Pickup

An active volunteer with appropriate vehicle capacity located in the donor's city/area is assigned to collect the surplus food batch.

Status: Pickup Assigned
4
Step 4: Pickup Acceptance & Transit
Volunteer Accepts & Collects Food

The volunteer accepts the assignment, travels to the donor pickup address, verifies the packaged quantity, and confirms pickup in the application.

Status: Pickup Accepted Status: Picked Up
5
Step 5: Handover to NGO
Delivery to Verified NGO Center

Food is delivered safely to the designated NGO distribution center or community kitchen, where it is inspected for proper temperature and condition.

Status: Delivered
6
Step 6: Community Distribution
Distribution to Beneficiaries & Completion

The NGO distributes meals directly to registered beneficiary families or individuals. Quantity reconciliation is logged and the donation lifecycle is closed.

Status: Distributed Final Status: Completed
Strict Workflow Guardrails
  • Completed donations cannot be edited or modified.
  • Expired donations are locked and cannot be accepted.
  • Cancelled donations cannot be assigned to volunteers.
  • Pickups cannot be marked completed before acceptance.
  • Distributions cannot occur prior to verified pickup.
  • Pickup quantity can never exceed available donation quantity.
